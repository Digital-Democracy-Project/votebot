VoteBot's `openstates_headers()` sends `Authorization: Bearer <DDP_OPENSTATES_BEARER_TOKEN>` when `USE_DDP_OPENSTATES_REPLICA=true` and `x-api-key` only when it is false. I also tried the broker's own configured `DDP_OPENSTATES_BEARER_TOKEN` (the broker's `DDP_OPENSTATES_API_ROOT` is the same `http://10.0.0.11:8002`) as Bearer on `/people`, `/bills` and `/ddp/search/suggest`: all **403**, the same as with no credential. So as things stand, replica mode on this host's api-v3 would be refused. Either VoteBot needs to reach a different URL that accepts a Bearer token (the public ddp-api route I used for a latency measurement on 10-05 did accept one; I do not have its URL in front of me and have not re-tested it), or the api-v3 here needs to accept Bearer, or VoteBot should send `X-API-Key`.
